description: "Pricing, prepaid credit, and payment guidance for the Octogen Catalog API."
---

This guide applies to prepaid Developer organizations using the covered Octogen Catalog APIs. If your organization has a separate contract, your agreed terms apply.

## API request prices

Prices are in USD.

| API request | Price (USD) per successful request |
| --- | --- |
| Product search | &#36;0.014 (&#36;14 per 1,000 requests) |
| Product lookup and parse | &#36;0.002 (&#36;2 per 1,000 requests) |

Successful HTTP 2xx requests are charged, including empty results. Each successful attempt is charged separately, including repeats, retries, and pagination. Non-2xx responses are free.

## Monthly free credit

Your Developer organization receives $5 in free credit each monthly renewal. Credit is shared across the organization’s API keys, and free credit is used first. Unused free credit expires at renewal. Buying credit does not change your renewal date or add another free-credit grant.

## Add credit

In Octogen Platform, open **Usage & billing**. Under **Payment method**, select **Add card**, then **Verify card**. Under **Add credits**, select **Add credits** to open **Buy credits**. Choose at least $10 and select **Continue**. Review the one-time charge and select **Pay**. Saving a card does not buy credit. There are no subscriptions or automatic recharges. Purchased credit does not expire and is non-refundable. It becomes available after successful payment.

## Balance and access

Balances and usage can take a few minutes to update. Delayed usage may use some of a new purchase, so your available balance may be lower than the amount you bought.

If prepaid credit is insufficient, covered API requests return HTTP 402 and are not charged. Add credit or wait for the next monthly renewal. Access resumes when enough credit is available; a purchase may not restore access if usage has consumed the available credit.

For a pending or failed payment, check **Purchase history** in **Usage & billing**. If the outcome is unknown, wait for its status instead of starting another purchase. For unresolved payment issues, contact [Octogen support](mailto:support@octogen.ai).
